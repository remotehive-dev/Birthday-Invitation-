A Message From Her Husband

Straight from the heart of Ranjeet Tiwari, for the love of his life

The day was 26th of April 2026 when I married her and made her my better half. From that day to this moment, whenever I look at her, I honestly feel how beautiful she is—she is truly the world's most beautiful lady. To be very honest, face-wise, feature-wise, and in every single way, I know I cannot beat her; I am not even close. But she still proudly wears me as her crown, and I treat her as my everything. I bow down to my princess... please be mine forever in this whole universe.

She is pure life, laughter, and madness! A complete night owl who loves going for late-night rides, exploring new places with the cool breeze in her hair. When it comes to food, she has her fixed golden rules: Corn & Cheese Pizza (strictly Domino's, nowhere else!) and a warm, gooey Choco Lava Cake that is her other true love and melts her heart every single time.

Put her in a black dress, and she looks like an angel walked straight down from heaven. And when any good music plays, she is the first to dance and shake the whole floor with her infectious joy!

An absolute Instagram lover, forever the darling princess of the Tripathis, and the heartbeat and everything of Ranjeet Tiwari.

"Thank you so much, my Princess. Happy Birthday, my love! ❤️"

— Forever yours, Ranjeet Tiwari 💕
